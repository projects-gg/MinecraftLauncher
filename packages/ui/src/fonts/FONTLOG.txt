FONTLOG for the launcher's Figtree and Tektur latin subsets
==========================================================

figtree-latin-wght-normal.woff2 and tektur-latin-wght-normal.woff2 are the text and display faces
of Projects Launcher and Projects Studio (@pl/ui, docs/design.md §3).

Source
------
Figtree (Copyright 2022 The Figtree Project Authors, https://github.com/erikdkennedy/figtree) and
Tektur (Copyright 2023 The Tektur Project Authors, https://www.github.com/hyvyys/Tektur), both under
the SIL Open Font License 1.1 with no Reserved Font Name (OFL-Figtree.txt, OFL-Tektur.txt), as
packaged by fontsource: @fontsource-variable/figtree and @fontsource-variable/tektur, the latin
subset of each variable font (fonts.json names the versions and the source files' SHA-256).

Changes (25 Oct 2026, Projects)
-------------------------------
- One pair-positioning lookup added to each font's "kern" feature: "r" followed by dotless "ı"
  (U+0131) moves the "ı" 70 units right in Figtree and 80 in Tektur (1000 units per em). Turkish
  writes "rı" in most plurals and possessives; the arm of "r" ends 0.07-0.09 em before the stem of
  "ı" and neither font kerned the pair, so at 12-17 px antialiasing joined them and "rı" read "n"
  ("Arkadaşlarında" as "Arkadaşlannda").
- Tektur only: the capital "Y" meets its stem higher. Its four shoulder points move from 210 to
  270 units at the weight axis's default (400) and to 370 at its maximum (900), 310 at the titles'
  600, always under the arms' inner junction; the outer two slide along their arm's edge, so the
  arms keep their slope and width. The stem was 0.21 em tall, two or three pixels under a wide V
  at 13-17 px, and "Yama notları" read "Vama notları". Advance, side bearings and kerning are the
  source's.
- Nothing else changed: every other glyph, metric and kerning value is the source's.
- The version strings (name IDs 3 and 5) end in "Projects build: r + dotless i kerning" (Figtree)
  and "Projects build: r + dotless i kerning fading with optical size, longer Y stem" (Tektur).

Changes (27 Oct 2026, Projects)
-------------------------------
- Tektur only: the "r" + dotless "ı" pair fades out over display sizes. At 22 px and up
  antialiasing no longer closes the arm's gap, and +80 set the "ı" 3-5 px apart in titles. The
  font gains an optical size axis ("opsz", 6 to 22, default 12, name "Optical size" in fvar and
  STAT) that moves nothing but this pair: +80 up to 17, then linearly less, 0 from 22 (one region
  on the axis in GDEF's variation store). Every existing variation region ignores the new axis;
  outlines, advances and the font's own kerning are unchanged at every weight and size. Browsers
  set opsz from the CSS font size by themselves (font-optical-sizing: auto).

The families keep their names (no Reserved Font Name). The other subsets the launcher loads
(latin-ext and, for Tektur, Cyrillic, Greek and Vietnamese) are fontsource's files, unchanged.

Build
-----
packages/ui/scripts/build-text-fonts.py (fontTools 4.67, brotli) from the installed fontsource
packages; it writes the two files and fonts.json. Run it again after upgrading either package.
Another fontTools or brotli release can write the same font with other bytes (the 27 Oct build
kept Figtree's file of 25 Oct: its tables decode alike).
